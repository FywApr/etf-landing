  1. Scope and audience

  The audience is Czech retail investors arriving on mobile from paid ads, who

  - One landing page with two entry variants, one per ad.

  2. Stack

  The stack is unchanged: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, Neon

  so the guide is generated from the same data file as the page and the two
  cannot drift apart.

  3. Lead magnet

  The offer is "Průvodce americkými ETF pro české investory", a PDF containing:
  - the extended comparison, with more fields per fund than the page shows;
  - an explanation of EU access limits;
  - a practical checklist for choosing an ETF.

  Delivery works without an email service:
  - A successful submission creates the lead with a random guideToken and
    redirects to /dekujeme?t=….
  - The download route checks the token against the database and streams the
    PDF.
  - The PDF is not in public/, so it cannot be fetched without a lead.

  4. Interactive fee calculator

  - Inputs: initial deposit, monthly contribution, horizon in years, assumed
    annual return, and one or two ETFs from the list.
  - Outputs: final value per fund, the amount lost to fees, and the difference
    between the two funds.
  - Formula: shown on the page and implemented as one pure function in
    lib/calculator.ts.
    - i = (1 + r − TER)^(1/12) − 1
    - FV = P(1+i)^n + C·((1+i)^n − 1)/i
    - feeCost = FV(TER=0) − FV(TER)
  - Labelling: the return is a user assumption, not a forecast. The page states
    that the result ignores taxes, FX, broker fees and spreads.
  - Tests: zero return, zero fee, a hand-computed reference value, and fee cost
    rising with TER.

  5. Ads and visitor journey

  8. Privacy and validation

  - Validation: the server action validates with Zod, normalises the email, and
    uses a honeypot plus a minimum fill time against spam.
  - Persistence: the lead and lead_created are written in one transaction. On
    failure the visitor sees an error and gets no guide access.
  - Duplicates: a repeat email gets the same response and guide access, without
    revealing that it exists.
  - Two purposes: the email is required for guide access. Marketing is a
    separate, unticked, optional checkbox, stored with a timestamp and the
    consent text version.
  - Controller details: the privacy page reads them from environment variables.
    If they are missing it shows a visible "to be completed" notice, and the
    README lists this as a launch blocker. Nothing is invented.
